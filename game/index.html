SOLMAR BAY

NEON TIDE · AN OPEN-WORLD CRIME STORY

You are Lena “Vex” Marlowe, a courier who just burned her last bridge in Solmar Bay — a sun-soaked port city of boardwalks, glass towers and bad decisions. Her brother Dario owes $40,000 to the Harlow crew, and the only way out is through the city’s jobs: drive, deliver, steal, fight — and stay ahead of the Solmar Bay Police Department. Ten jobs. One debt. No way back but forward.

F / Click — punch / shoot
Shift — sprint
