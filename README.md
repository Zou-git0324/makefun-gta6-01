Solmar Bay is a 3D open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Make GTA 6” bounty, $1,666 in USDC. You play Lena "Vex" Marlowe, a courier in a sun-soaked port city who has six jobs to pay off her brother's $40,000 debt to the Harlow crew. Explore the city on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the Solmar Bay Police Department's wanted system, fight, and finish the story — with a HUD, minimap, missions, combat, save games and synthesised sound. Everything is original: the city, the characters, the story and the art. It runs fully offline from its own files.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```

Controls: WASD to move/drive, mouse drag to look, E to enter/steal a car, F or click to punch/shoot, Shift to sprint, Space for the handbrake, P to pause and save, M to mute.
