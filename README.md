Solmar Bay is a 3D open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Make GTA 6” bounty, $1,666 in USDC. You play Lena "Vex" Marlowe, a courier in a sun-soaked port city who has ten jobs to pay off her brother's $40,000 debt to the Harlow crew — six story jobs, then four extra jobs after the debt is paid. Explore the city on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the Solmar Bay Police Department's wanted system (police now PIT-ram your car, flash red/blue light bars, and send a helicopter with a searchlight at four stars), fight, and finish the story — with a HUD, minimap, job progress and distance readout, missions (including timed checkpoint runs and dead-drop collection jobs), combat, save games, synthesised sound, and a full day/dusk/night lighting cycle. Cars come in kinds — sedans, sports cars, coupes, vans and taxis — each with its own handling and top speed, and crashes bend them: damage smokes the hood and eats top speed, shown on the speedometer. Everything is original: the city, the characters, the story and the art. It runs fully offline from its own files.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```

Controls: WASD to move/drive, mouse drag to look, E to enter/steal a car, F or click to punch/shoot, Shift to sprint, Space for the handbrake, P to pause and save, M to mute.
